Scam Awareness

Slow down, verify the details, and protect your money and identity.

Common Warning Signs

  • Guaranteed returns or unusually high pay for little effort.
  • Pressure to act immediately or keep an opportunity secret.
  • Requests for upfront fees, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or bank credentials.
  • Vague business details, unverifiable testimonials, or suspicious contact addresses.

Before You Commit

Search independently, verify the organization using trusted sources, read the terms, and ask someone you trust for a second opinion. A legitimate opportunity can withstand careful questions.

Remember: Legitimate employers and educators do not require payment to release promised earnings or secure a job.

Report and Protect Yourself

Stop communicating, preserve messages and receipts, contact your bank quickly if money was sent, and report suspected fraud to the relevant authorities in your country.
